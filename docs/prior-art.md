# Prior Art and Differentiation

## Overlap intentionally not claimed as invention

- Strands Human-in-the-Loop and tool approval already support pausing for people.
- Agent firewalls and governance-by-construction systems already gate high-risk calls.
- Delegation/capability systems already represent revocable authority and least privilege.
- Workflow engines and transactional systems already support compensation/rollback patterns.
- Oversight-capacity research already shows that reviewer load and fatigue can be safety variables.
- Dynamic approval-routing systems and patent literature predate this project.

These overlaps eliminate a defensible claim that Authority Cut invented HITL, approval routing, revocation or rollback.

## Candidate contribution

Authority Cut represents protected workflow effects as required **semantic authority atoms**, restricts optimization to policy-approved decision bundles, computes the exact minimum cover, binds approvals to prerequisite receipts, keeps approval/revocation outside the model-callable tool set, and propagates a later principal correction through executed reversible descendants.

The strongest competition evidence is therefore not “we ask for approval.” It is the observable 7 protected effects -> 3 semantic decisions -> selective compensation path under a strict external-authority boundary.

## Novelty confidence

**MEDIUM as a competition/product primitive; LOW-MEDIUM as a broad research/patent novelty claim.** Prior-art review was substantial but not exhaustive legal freedom-to-operate analysis. No “first”, “unique” or patentability claim should appear in submission copy.
